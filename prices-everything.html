Prices in the Patent Rolls

Everything the rolls price

99,114 entries in the Patent, Close, Fine, Charter and Liberate Rolls name a sum; 56,766 read as a price or a payment. A first sort by rules: the kind from the words an act opens with, the sum from its money words, and whether it was paid once, a day or a year. Not yet read by hand; the two linked kinds have been, and have pages of their own.

The sums not read as a price

42,348 entries name a sum the rules do not read as a price or payment. The first such sum is:

  • 23,845: no price word before it
  • 9,858: the value or rent of land
  • 3,966: under 3s. 4d.
  • 3,669: a fee, wage, debt or penalty word before it
  • 1,010: a wage by the day, foreign money or a debt still owed

Values of land include the land given to churches that mortmain licences state; they are a valuation, not a price, and come next, with every sum's part named. The medians are in marks (13s. 4d.); a row's line is its median by 50 years, where 5 or more were priced, on one scale for every row.
